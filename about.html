Who We Are

Built on vision,
grounded in craft

Cactus was founded with a singular goal — to elevate the standards of modern architecture. What began as a small team of forward-thinking designers has grown into a respected studio, known for balancing bold aesthetics with honest functionality.

Over the years, we have honed our craft, collaborating with clients across Asia and beyond to create spaces that inspire. Our dedication to sustainability, innovation, and human-centred design guides everything we do.

18+ Years active
240 Projects built
34 Awards won

"We design with purpose — every project is an opportunity to improve the world around us."

By focusing on sustainable practices and cutting-edge materials, we create structures that serve the present and respect the future. Our passion for innovation is reflected in every detail, ensuring our designs stand the test of time.

We believe in creating spaces that are not only beautiful but also environmentally responsible. By integrating green building practices into every project, we ensure our designs minimise impact on the planet while maximising comfort and well-being.

Our Journey

A decade of milestones

2006

Studio Founded

Wei Lin establishes Cactus Architecture Studio in Kuala Lumpur with a team of three architects and a bold ambition to redefine Malaysian modernism.

2010

First Major Award

The studio wins the PAM Gold Award for the Riverine Residence — a watershed project that put Cactus on the regional design map.

2015

Regional Expansion

Cactus opens a second studio in Singapore, expanding its footprint across Southeast Asia and taking on landmark commercial commissions.

2019

Sustainability Pledge

The studio commits to net-zero carbon across all projects by 2030 — pioneering green building practices across every discipline.

2024

Today

With over 240 completed projects and a team of 40+ specialists, Cactus continues to shape the built environment across Asia and beyond.
